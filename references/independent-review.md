# Independent audit and skill calibration

Use this guidance when the user actually requests independence or a comparison. It does not mandate multiple agents or model voting for ordinary editing.

## Inputs and context

Use a fresh context with a neutral audience/task brief and the minimum complete raw artifacts. Preserve the starting document, actual rendered copy and skill version. Do not give the discovery reader historical review lists, your suspected fixes, a desired verdict or a finding quota. Supporting data/code are evidence, not an answer key. Log which files were actually opened; context separation in a shared workspace is not an OS access barrier.

If an unaided title/abstract impression is required, release only that opening first. Save/hash the reader's reconstruction before releasing the body and evidence in a follow-up. Merely asking a reader to look at the opening first, while handing it prior findings or the whole packet, provides a weaker claim. A later body-aware interpretation can resolve the initial questions without overwriting the original impression.

When source/render provenance is uncertain, build an authorized scratch copy through the real pipeline or mark the relationship unchecked. Different PDF bytes do not establish different content. Actual images are necessary for visual claims; if image-viewing capability is uncertain, verify it and retain tool/path/hash/observation receipts. Text extraction and a coverage ledger cannot establish legibility.

## Comparison and adjudication

A control reader should get the same raw evidence and project requirements, without the target skill's detailed procedure. Preserve the user's constraints but avoid copying the entire skill into the control brief. Do not claim the skill was disabled if only its text/helper access was prohibited; catalog descriptions may remain visible. Log any contamination and coordinator assistance. Single runs share model biases and do not identify a causal skill effect at matched computation.

Freeze original reports before comparing them. Normalize presentation/identity without rewriting the findings, and preserve the raw reports and private mapping. An independent evaluator checks claims and complete proposed replacements against context/evidence, including combinations. More findings, agreement, shorter wording or a clean tool result is not a quality score. Separate supported defects, useful clarity, optional author choices, harmful repairs and unavailable checks. Without independent gold, report supported discoveries and omissions, not true recall.

A reviewer that receives paper and repair list in the same packet is primed, regardless of their order. Attribute any additional discoveries accordingly; never retroactively credit them to the original audit. Truly unprimed discovery needs a paper-only pass frozen before the repair list is disclosed. Use that extra pass only when it resolves a material need, not merely to obtain another agreement signal.

Apply exact independently checked wording. Coordinator-authored combinations or surrounding rewrites that differ materially from reviewed text need a focused meaning/evidence check before delivery. Verification must follow real generation inputs through rendered and derived copies; a fixed phrase expected by an old checker is not authority for the paper's wording.

## Improving the skill

Diagnose a verified miss by its actual cause: input/provenance, extraction, method interpretation, reading scope, inappropriate severity, or helper mechanics. A precise local instruction may suffice; do not add a new universal workflow for every example. A mature helper need not change merely because a document supplied useful editorial lessons.

Add positive as well as negative regression cases. Have fresh text chosen independently without showing the fixture author the paper-specific fix, then freeze the revised skill before the evaluating reader sees that text. The corrected paper is development evidence. Targeted source-assisted probes can establish a mechanism check; they do not prove broad editorial superiority, author satisfaction, novelty or publication readiness.
